Before you buy

Is this a Christian book? Will it argue against my faith?

It's written for believers and respects the Bible as Scripture. We show what each object is and what it shows, and we are honest where scholars disagree. We never argue against faith or miracles, and we never claim a stone 'proves' the Bible.

How large is the print?

The notes are set in 17-point type and the Bible verses in 17-point serif, on full 8.5 × 11-inch pages. The photographs fill half a page. You can read it without your glasses on more than you'd think.
